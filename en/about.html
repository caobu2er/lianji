Story

Describe the cloth first. Then talk partnership.

Lianji started in the Keqiao fabric market, serving Jiangsu and Zhejiang garment factories. As designer brands grew, we moved the showroom to Shanghai and turned specs, color cards and lead times into a fixed process so small lots and bulk share one standard.

How we work

Three things decide whether a partnership runs cleanly.

01

Visible

Weight, width, composition, colorfastness and shrinkage sit on every quote. Nothing is “about right”.

02

Restrained

No extra concepts, no surplus stock push. We recommend only what fits this season and this style.

03

Traceable

Yarn lots, dye-vat numbers and QC sheets can be traced. If something fails, we locate the roll.
