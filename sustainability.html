Three principles

Cutting waste at the order is more effective than compensating later.

MATERIAL

Natural and recycled first

Cotton, linen, silk and wool are the default. Recycled synthetics enter when function requires them.

PROCESS

Lab-dip before bulk

Bulk does not start until shade and hand pass. That reduces dye-lot waste and leftover inventory.

CYCLE

Offcuts stay visible

Leftover bulk can be bought back or moved into next-season stock instead of sitting in a warehouse.
